About Us

Welcome to Haomics Lab, founded by Xingjie Hao (郝兴杰) at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST, 华中科技大学). We are an interdisciplinary research group specializing in statistical genomics, multi-omics, artificial intelligence, and population health.

We develop novel statistical models, scalable computational algorithms, and bioinformatics tools for polygenic risk scoring, cross-population genetic analysis, fine-mapping, gene–environment interaction, causal inference, and linear and nonlinear omics association. By combining rigorous statistical frameworks with statistical learning and artificial intelligence, we address challenges arising from high-dimensional data, population heterogeneity, correlated molecular traits, and prediction uncertainty.

We integrate GWAS, TWAS, PWAS, Mendelian randomization, proteomics, metabolomics, epigenomics, and single-cell data to connect genetic variation with molecular traits, tissues and cell types, and complex diseases. Our work has advanced annotation-informed risk prediction, trans-ancestry genetic studies, and nonlinear proteome-wide association analysis, with applications to cardiovascular, metabolic, urinary, neurological, and other complex disorders.

We warmly welcome opportunities for academic and interdisciplinary collaboration, as well as motivated students who share our scientific interests and would like to grow with the lab.
